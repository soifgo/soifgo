About SoifGo — IoT Controller App for Android

Developer: Saeid Moghadam

Saeid Moghadam is the creator of SoifGo, a powerful and multilingual IoT controller for Android.

What is SoifGo?

SoifGo is an Android application that provides users with advanced capabilities using buttons and seek bars. It supports Bluetooth and MQTT protocols, making it ideal for controlling ESP32, Arduino, HC-05, and ESP8266 devices.

Key Features

  • Control ESP32 via Bluetooth from Android
  • Connect to HC-05 and HC-06 modules
  • MQTT protocol support for internet communication
  • Buttons and seek bars for precise control
  • 22 languages supported
  • Perfect for Arduino, ESP8266, and robotics projects
  • Two-way communication with IoT devices
  • Ideal for home automation and industrial projects

Frequently Asked Questions

How to connect ESP32 to Android via Bluetooth?

SoifGo allows you to easily connect and control ESP32 via Bluetooth. Install the app, pair your device, and start sending commands through buttons and seek bars.

What devices does SoifGo support?

SoifGo supports ESP32, Arduino, HC-05, HC-06, ESP8266, and any device that communicates via Bluetooth SPP or MQTT protocol.

Is SoifGo free and open-source?

Yes! SoifGo is completely free to download and use. The source code is available for developers who want to contribute or customize it.

Can I use SoifGo for robotics projects?

Absolutely! SoifGo is perfect for controlling robots with Bluetooth modules like HC-05 and HC-06. Use buttons for movement and seek bars for speed control.

Does SoifGo support MQTT?

Yes, SoifGo includes MQTT support, allowing you to control your IoT devices over the internet in addition to Bluetooth.

What languages does SoifGo support?

SoifGo supports 22 languages including English, Spanish, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Russian, French, German, Japanese, Persian, and many more.

How to control Arduino with Bluetooth from Android?

Install SoifGo, pair with your Arduino's HC-05/06 module, and use the buttons and seek bars to send commands.

Is SoifGo available on Google Play?

SoifGo is currently available as a direct download (APK) and on APKPure. Google Play release is coming soon.
