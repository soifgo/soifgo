🔒 Privacy Policy for Soifgo

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

1. Information Collection

Soifgo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data. The application operates entirely offline and does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

2. Data Usage

The app processes only user-created project files, which are stored locally on the device. No data is ever sent to external servers or third parties.

3. Required Permissions

  • Storage: To save and load project files, themes, and exported assets.
  • Internet (optional): Only if you choose to sync projects across devices (if this feature is implemented).

4. Data Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of personal data exposure. All project files remain solely on the user's device.

5. Third-Party Links

The app may contain links to external sites (e.g., download pages). These sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.
